Historical research snapshot. The implemented watcher and its current configuration are documented in [Reset alerts](../reset-alerts.md); setup-status statements below describe the research stage.

Research date: 12 September 2026. Account: [Tibo / @thsottiaux](https://x.com/thsottiaux).

Located **91 relevant posts and replies**, including **74 explicit announcements, confirmations, or timing updates**, plus **17 hints, policy discussions, historical references, or replies requiring context**. These are post counts, not counts of distinct reset events. Coverage in this collection runs from 17 September 2025 to 12 September 2026. This is the complete list located in this research, not a claim to contain every post ever published. No authenticated full-account archive was available; deleted posts, unindexed replies, older omissions, and activity on other social networks may be missing.

X's public oEmbed endpoint returned the author and post text for all 91 IDs. 89 excerpts below were checked directly against that text. Two long-post excerpts were recovered from linked mirrors because the official embed truncates before the reset sentence; their authors and beginnings were independently checked. An ellipsis or absent surrounding text is not silently completed. All excerpts are at most 25 words; they are not full copies of long posts. Open the date link for the original post.

Times below are **UTC publication times**, decoded from the original X post ID. They are not rollout timestamps. The accompanying JSON also includes Europe/London times. Some announcement wording says PST during daylight saving time; keep that original wording and flag the ambiguity rather than silently choosing an offset.

## Recommended detection

1. **Collect the author's new posts and replies.** Fetch Tibo's X user-post timeline every five minutes, excluding reposts but preserving replies and quote posts. Persist the last seen ID and paginate after downtime. Capture full long-post text and referenced parent/quoted posts when available. The official API supports user timelines and a since_id cursor. A filtered stream using from:thsottiaux can reduce latency. Both routes need the appropriate X API account/access. [X timeline documentation](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/timelines/introduction), [filtered stream](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/filtered-stream/introduction).
2. **Use a broad text filter.** Match reset and spelling variants (resetting, reseting, reseted), banked, reset bank, usage/rate limits, replenished, fresh/new usage, button presses and rollout wording. Include hints, questions, jokes, negations, policy discussions and banked-reset explanations. False positives are acceptable; do not require a confirmed reset or even the word Codex. An AI classifier is optional and must not be a gate that suppresses relevant posts.
3. **Include relevant replies and quotes.** Forward Tibo's short replies when their parent or quoted post is reset-related, even if his own text only says yes or done. Retain the link to Tibo's reply so the recipient can inspect the conversation. A reset-related parent establishes relevance, not proof that a reset occurred.
4. **Send a neutral alert with a link.** Label it “Tibo posted about resets”, followed by a short excerpt, publication time and the original post URL. Avoid asserting that the user's allowance has reset. The recipient will interpret the announcement. A banked/automatic classification and estimated time are optional context, not requirements for delivery.
5. **Deduplicate repeated retrievals of the same post.** Persist post IDs and handle edit history. Separate follow-ups, timing corrections and confirmations are useful and may each produce an alert even if they concern one reset event. First startup should establish a baseline instead of replaying the whole archive.
6. **Make failures visible.** Use timeouts, backoff, Retry-After, and a source-health state. A failed fetch means unknown, not no reset. Five-minute polling is 288 checks/day and about 2.5 minutes average detection delay after a post is available to the source, plus processing and delivery time. It cannot guarantee five-minute detection if the upstream feed is stale.

## Lowest-setup alternative

[Codex Resets](https://codex-resets.com/api/docs) exposes unauthenticated read-only status and history endpoints. In this research both worked. The status response separates latest_reset, scheduled_reset and active_watch; the schema supports ETag conditional requests and Retry-After. This could be checked every five minutes, then passed to Dobby with the source post link. Include newly linked scheduled/watch posts as well as completed resets; a changing forecast probability alone is not a new post. It adds an independent tracker's detection delay and cannot guarantee complete coverage. Its current history mislabels the July 12 and July 13 banked grants as regular, and its two-type schema does not express the June 18 and June 29 combined grants. These label errors matter less for neutral link alerts, but omitted posts remain a limitation.

Prefer direct X collection for dependable coverage; use this public API as a low-setup pilot or corroborating feed. [NextReset's methodology](https://nextreset.ai/guide/) explicitly describes its own history as selected and manually curated, so its RSS is unsuitable as the sole source for a five-minute target.

## WhatsApp integration in this workspace

The existing nftomorrow/Dobby service already has WhatsApp sending code and persisted delivery bookkeeping. A reset watcher could run beside the current NFT checks and send alerts to the owner's private chat, using a separately configured recipient. Research inspected README.md, src/config.js, src/service.js and src/whatsapp.js references; it did not test a live WhatsApp connection or send a message. No watcher, scheduled task, production change, or alert subscription was activated.

Proposed alert fields: neutral title, short excerpt, publication time, and original post link. Example title: “Tibo posted about resets”. Optional classification must not delay or prevent delivery.

The implementation choices still needed are the source (direct X API versus the public tracker pilot) and the destination private chat. A small polling component can do the fetching, broad text filtering and bookkeeping without an AI call on every check. This update records the user's detection preference; monitoring remains inactive.

No new first-party reset posts on LinkedIn or Bluesky were established in the searches. Third-party LinkedIn posts led back to Tibo's X account. This supports starting with X; it does not establish that he never announces elsewhere.
